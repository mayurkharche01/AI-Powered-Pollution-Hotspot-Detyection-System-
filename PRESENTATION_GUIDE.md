# CleanAir & Clear Streets — Hackathon Pitch Deck Outline
**Designed for Google Build with AI, Google Solution Challenge, & Microsoft Imagine Cup**

---

## SLIDE 1: Title & The Vision
*   **Visual Layout Idea:** Premium minimalist dark background. Large elegant white typography with a glowing sky-cyan and emerald-green gradient.
*   **Slide Header:** **CleanAir & Clear Streets**
*   **Sub-heading / Tagline:** *AI-powered Hyperlocal Pollution Intelligence Platform*
*   **Key Talking Points:**
    *   Introduce yourselves and set the tone: This is not just a dashboard; it is an active urban cognitive defense grid.
    *   **The Hook:** "Traditional sensors monitor the sky miles away, but communities breathe at street level. Today, we bridge that gap."

---

## SLIDE 2: The Invisible Killer (The Problem)
*   **Visual Layout Idea:** Split slide. Left side has high-contrast red warning bullet points. Right side shows a schematic of a city map with huge blindspots between official air stations.
*   **Slide Title:** **Our Cities Breathe in Blindspots**
*   **Key Metrics / Bullets:**
    *   **Coarse AQI Data:** City-wide sensors are placed 10–15 miles apart, completely missing local high-toxicity spikes.
    *   **Invisible Triggers:** Spontaneous landfill fires, illegal midnight waste burning, construction silica dust, and traffic exhaust bottlenecks.
    *   **The Cost:** Chronic respiratory disease, childhood asthma clusters, and billions in productivity loss.
*   **Presenter Note:** "When your standard weather app says the air is 'Good' (green), there could be a plastic waste fire burning 200 meters from a public playground. Legacy systems are completely blind to this."

---

## SLIDE 3: The Paradigm Shift (Our Solution)
*   **Visual Layout Idea:** Beautiful bento-box diagram representing the three core dimensions of our platform: Citizen-Led, Satellite-Verified, and IoT-Hardened.
*   **Slide Title:** **Sovereign Multi-Spectral Intelligence**
*   **Key Pillars:**
    *   **Citizen Reports:** Instant geo-tagged image uploads processed by Computer Vision in 3 seconds.
    *   **Satellite Remote Sensing:** Real-time retrieval of Copernicus Sentinel-5P gas columns (NO2, SO2, CO) and Landsat thermal data.
    *   **Edge IoT Mesh:** Distributed, low-cost particulate monitors measuring ground-level PM2.5 and PM10 continuously.

---

## SLIDE 4: The Intelligent Pipeline (How it Works)
*   **Visual Layout Idea:** A sleek horizontal step-by-step pipeline diagram tracing the flow of data.
*   **Slide Title:** **The Cognitive Fusion Pipeline**
*   **Pipeline Steps:**
    1.  **Ingestion:** Citizen Upload → Satellite Remote Sensing → Edge IoT Feeds.
    2.  **Analysis:** Vertex AI Computer Vision classifies plumes → Weather matrix models wind dispersals.
    3.  **Core Fusion:** Gemini 3.5-Flash correlates multi-layered vectors to verify hotspot legitimacy and compute confidence.
    4.  **Municipal Dispatch:** Instant Webhook sends actionable coordinates, estimated AQI, and suppressive guides to local authorities.

---

## SLIDE 5: The AI Core (Multi-Modal Gemini Ingestion)
*   **Visual Layout Idea:** Show a screenshot of the Citizen Report Panel alongside the structured JSON output returned by Gemini.
*   **Slide Title:** **Generative Multi-Modal Fusion Engine**
*   **Under the Hood:**
    *   **Gemini 3.5-Flash:** Ingests unformatted natural text and optional JPG attachments on the server-side.
    *   **Spectroscopic Inference:** Classifies emission types (e.g., polyurethane combustion, diesel particulate traps, raw concrete dust) based on visual smoke density and description.
    *   **Adaptive Recommendation:** Generates real-time, site-specific guides for municipal dispatchers (e.g., "Deploy fire suppressive foam," "Activate smart mist-sprays").

---

## SLIDE 6: 3D Cognitive Grid Layer (The Live HUD)
*   **Visual Layout Idea:** Showcase the real-time interactive 3D City Map with glowing red pulsing hotspot coordinates and color-mapped heatmap tiles.
*   **Slide Title:** **10-Meter Street-Level Resolution**
*   **Key Innovations:**
    *   **Micro-Climates:** Maps air stagnation corridors where toxic plumes get trapped by local building heights.
    *   **Interactive HUD:** Allows municipal operators to click any active coordinate node and run multi-spectral analysis instantly.
    *   **Dynamic Heatmapping:** Moves away from smooth gradients to strict cellular grids, indicating precise localized exposure zones.

---

## SLIDE 7: 24-Hour Predictive Dispersion Models
*   **Visual Layout Idea:** A high-contrast chart showing two curves: "No Action" (rapid pollution buildup) vs. "Suppressed" (municipal intervention mitigates toxicity).
*   **Slide Title:** **Predictive Air Defense**
*   **Key Capabilities:**
    *   **Thermodynamic Modeling:** Combines wind velocity, humidity, and temperature gradients to forecast where a plume will travel in the next 24 hours.
    *   **Proactive Suppression:** Alerts cities *before* toxic thresholds are breached, enabling dynamic traffic rerouting or construction water-sprinkler triggers.
